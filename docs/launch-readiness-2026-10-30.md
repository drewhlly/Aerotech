# Launch readiness and Nigerian legal review — 30 October 2026

This is an engineering and policy gap review, not a legal opinion, compliance certificate, tax filing or professional sign-off. A qualified Nigerian lawyer/data-protection professional and payroll adviser must approve the applicable operational arrangements. Do not treat passing software tests as proof of legal compliance.

## Changes and evidence

- Salary payments show the company name, encryption configuration, account mode, active approval-stage assignments and missing bank verification. Setup completion never authorizes a transfer. Employer-specific credentials remain separate from Aerotech subscription collection.
- Foreign-origin preparation, recipient creation and reconciliation requests are rejected. CEO authorization, exact totals, account fingerprints, stable transfer references, signed callbacks and reconciliation remain mandatory.
- NHF preview uses 2.5% of monthly income when enabled, replacing the former basic-only base. Private-sector participation must be established and documented before enabling it. Tax/rent-relief and pension previews require employee-specific evidence and payroll-professional validation.
- Privacy, Terms and Cookie notices are versioned 2026-10-30.1. The contact is support@aerotechhr.app. Unknown legal-entity, DPO, retention and commercial facts are expressly marked [EDIT]. Existing acceptance logs remain historical evidence; do not rewrite them as acceptance of the new version.
- Automated rehearsal covers employer/employee/applicant sessions, tenant and portal isolation, Aerochat exchange, interview permissions, approvals, bank-change protection, signed transfer callbacks, privacy requests and consent. Meeting-provider UI is simulated in CI: real two-device audio/video, device permissions and network quality remain a separate acceptance check.
- No production payroll transfer or customer-bank change is part of this release. No production migration is required.

## Rehearsal acceptance

1. Use a disposable isolated database, employer and candidate accounts; never populate customer tenants with fixture staff or bank details.
2. Verify all three dedicated sign-ins; unpaid employer gating; role-appropriate navigation and denied cross-portal/API access.
3. Publish a lawful test role; upload a CV; complete assessment; progress through an employer review and interview; check candidate permissions.
4. Send staff Aerochat messages in both directions; confirm outsiders cannot read them. Simulate provider UI only in CI.
5. Connect a test-mode company account, verify designated test recipients, complete all three approvals, prepare once and verify stable references. Test foreign-origin denial, bank-detail changes, invalid signatures, duplicate callbacks, failed/reversed states and reconciliation without real money.
6. Confirm new policy version at registration, optional cookies off until chosen, tracked privacy requests, restricted exports and audit entries.
7. With two real participants on separate devices/networks, test actual JaaS join, microphone/camera controls, audio both ways, disconnect/rejoin, interview access and room termination. Record timestamp, devices and result without retaining unnecessary audio/video.

## Primary reference register

- [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 — NDPC](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf), particularly notices/rights, DPO obligations, automated decisions, security/incidents and transfers. Confirm current GAID 2025 through [NDPC](https://ndpc.gov.ng/); the final PDF was blocked during this review, so no draft directive is represented as current final law.
- [Nigeria Tax Act 2025 — NIPC](https://www.nipc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Nigeria-Tax-Act-2025.pdf); [2026 transition guidance — Federal Ministry of Finance](https://finance.gov.ng/ministry/federal-government-issues-transition-guidelines-for-tax-acts-2025/); [JRB 2026 PIT guidance notice](https://www.jtb.gov.ng/media-center/jrb-releases-pit-guidelines-2026).
- [Business Facilitation Act 2022 — official Gazette via NIPC](https://nipc.gov.ng/assets/resources/business-facilitation-miscellaneous-provisions-act-2022/business-facilitation-misc-provisions-act-2022.pdf), Schedule sections 45–46 (NHF amendments); [current FMBN FAQ](https://fmbn.gov.ng/products/nhf-scheme/faqs).
- [Pension scope — PenCom](https://www.pencom.gov.ng/framework-for-micro-pension-plan/); obtain the applicable Pension Reform Act and current remittance guidance before production statutory sign-off.
- [Labour Act — ILO NATLEX register](https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/r/natlex/fe/details?p3_isn=42156). Apply the Act's worker definitions and relevant exceptions; do not assume every contractual classification has identical entitlements.
